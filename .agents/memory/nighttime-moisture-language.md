# Nighttime moisture language

Section 4 may use separate derived signals for condensation proximity and absolute moisture to distinguish a cool damp night from a muggy one. Give the interpreter qualitative signals rather than raw dew points or temperature–dew-point spreads.

**Why:** Raw values prompted technical prose such as “Taupunkt-Spread unter 2 °C”, while the useful reader-facing conclusion was simply whether the night would feel damp, dry, or muggy.

**How to apply:** Mention nighttime moisture only when it adds meteorological value. Keep neutral conditions silent and reject explanations containing dew-point values, spreads, or calculation details.
